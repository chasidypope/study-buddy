What is Study Buddy? ♡

Study Buddy is a cozy student planner built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. It helps you organize assignments, explore deadlines on a calendar, and study with a simple focus timer.

Assignments, theme preferences, and completed focus-session counts are saved in this browser's local storage. Your tasks don't sync across devices, and this isn't a shared service or a notification app.
